**Definition:** A visitor-guide assistant robot born from everyday life. Hospital, factory, building lobby, department store service center, logistics center, general device service center (laptop/smartphone/camera), food court/restaurant unmanned concierge. From individual to home to field.

**Architecture:** [Brain] → [Governance: soma-moa] → [Actuator/APK]. Via Article X, regardless of form factor — wheeled/quadruped/humanoid/kiosk/hologram/snowball — governed identically with a 2-I/O requirement.

**When Stopped:** Charter 0 - Robot is subordinate, System is primary. Self-restart prohibited.
L1 BLE/MQTT-SN Diagnostics / L2 Lock & Telemetry Collection / L3 WebRTC Human Sign-off (100% blocked without Ed25519 signature, Cloud-Sign/Edge-Verify)

**Security:** Zero Trust (allowlisted networks only), RAM<10MB, CBOR<50B, 42KB SDK. APK blocked from install/run without Cloud-Sign, L2 Lock on repackaging/rooting.

**AS 3 Types:** Remote/OTA, On-site Dispatch, Resident Staff (stationed at hospitals/factories/department stores/logistics/device service/food courts. Daily checks, L1 diagnostics, L3 mediation. Execution only, not judgment)

**Personal Customization:** Medication/tasks/health routines, device repair history/warranty, diet/allergies. Finance after regulatory validation. Not a simple scheduler.

**Extended Discovery:** Librarian (find a book by plot/feeling/cover memory without title) + Music Discovery (find by humming/feeling/lyrical story). No guessing when uncertain, connect to L3.

**Safety:** Based on Heinrich 300/29/1 (Heinrich, 1931), extensible with Swiss Cheese (Reason, 1990), Defense in Depth, Fail-Safe, ALARP.

**Originality:** Someone else may have had the same idea. All standards are open standards. What is original is the governance structure I tied together from everyday life with Charter 0 and Article X. AI (Meta AI & Gemini) is used as a review tool.
